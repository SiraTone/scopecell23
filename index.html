What you get

KyCast covers 35.0-40.5 N, 91.0-80.4 W on a 0.03 degree grid, in 3-hour steps out to 5 days. The live viewer shows the latest published run and warns when it is out of date.

Reflectivity

A probability-matched, radar-style picture of where storms are likely, with balanced, crisp and chance-based views.

Tornado chance

Chance of a tornado within 25 miles, for the 12Z-12Z convective day or the 3 hours around each frame.

Nowcast, 0-2 hours

A live radar forecast rebuilt from NOAA MRMS every few minutes: where today's storms are heading over the next two hours, with a running score against real radar.

Honest limits

  • Few events: only 19 tornado days in the check. The numbers are noisy.
  • Tornado probabilities above about 15% were overconfident in testing, so a daily chance is never shown above 15%.
  • On neighbourhood overlap (fractions skill score, 33 km) of 25 dBZ reflectivity at day 1, raw GFS scored higher (0.46) than thresholded KyCast maps (0.25, at a 25% probability cut). KyCast's advantage is calibrated probabilities, not a sharper storm picture.
  • Reflectivity at days 3-5 is a statistical picture of where storms are possible, not a prediction of individual cells.
  • The reflectivity layer is derived from probabilities, not a physics simulation.
  • Trained on a limited sample of days (2021-2025) and one GFS version; behaviour in other regimes is unknown.
  • It was not compared with HRRR forecasts.
  • Experimental. Not an official forecast. Do not use it for safety decisions.
