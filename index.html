Our approach

A forecast is only as useful as its confidence.

Saying a storm will hit is easy. Knowing how often you are right when you say 30% is hard. We build models that learn where weather happens, then check and correct their own confidence on days they have never seen, so a number means what it says.

Principles

How we work.

  • Calibrated, not just confident.Every probability is corrected and scored out of sample before it is shown.
  • Honest numbers.We publish benchmarks against raw GFS, including the rows where we lose.
  • Small, focused models.Built for the next one to two days, where most decisions get made.
  • Independent.No agenda beyond forecasts you can reason about. Experimental, and labeled that way.
