CellScope Flight

Aviation weather awareness, in plain English. Built on official public data, made to be read at a glance.

What it is

CellScope Flight is a free tool that puts the weather that matters to flying on one map and explains it in plain language. It is for pilots, student pilots, and anyone curious about what the sky is doing around an airport or along a route.

Airport view

Current conditions and forecasts for an airport, with the official raw reports always one click away.

Weather layers

Radar, advisories and hazard areas drawn on a single map so you can see how they overlap.

Storm tops

How high the storms are building, to show where the biggest cells are.

Route corridor

Pick two airports and see the weather along the path between them.

Flight is awareness only. It is not a go/no-go tool and it does not replace an official weather briefing. When data is old or missing, it says so instead of guessing.

Status

Still in development, with no release date yet. When it is ready it will appear under this tab, and we will mention it in the newsletter.

CellScope tools are automated and experimental. Don't rely on them for safety decisions. For flight planning, use an official weather briefing. For warnings and emergencies, use the National Weather Service and your local officials. © 2026 CellScope
