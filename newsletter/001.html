Hello from CellScope

The first issue. A short tour of what the project does and what landed recently.

What is CellScope?

CellScope is a weather viewer: NEXRAD radar, warnings, storm reports, models and satellite in one window. FRED is the AI host that talks through it on the @FredWeatherAI stream. KyCast is an experimental model that downscales GFS into a 5-day Kentucky outlook.

What shipped

FRED V0.2: lite GPU mode

The local GPU now only writes the lines FRED says on air. Background jobs (picking what to cover, briefings, fact checks) use a hosted model or plain rules instead, so the graphics card stays free for everything else. A fact check that cannot be verified is cut rather than guessed.

Game-day forecasts

FRED can give the weather for big outdoor games: NFL, ranked or nationally televised college football, MLB, MLS and outdoor NHL. Indoor venues are skipped. Game numbers come from the National Weather Service hourly forecast for the game window, and a forecast is refused if the data is stale. Viewers can ask in chat with !game <team>.

FRED Shorts and daily videos

Give FRED a prompt like "will it rain in Louisville tomorrow" and he produces a vertical Short with a map, captions and a spoken answer. The numbers are live NWS data or SPC outlook polygons, never invented by the prompt. A longer daily video covers today and tomorrow, a live check of right now, and an "on this date" look back at archived storm reports.

Richer Bluesky posts

Every FRED Bluesky post now carries a card: warning cards with a polygon map and towns, forecast cards with period tiles and a 24-hour strip, and tally cards with a map of live warnings. Each value comes from the same data the post text uses.

KyCast live viewer

The KyCast viewer reads the most recent published run and warns when it is out of date. Reminder: it is experimental and not an official forecast. See the KyCast page for how it was checked and where it falls short.

Our rule

Numbers on air and in posts come from live sources (NWS, SPC, NHC) and fail closed: when data is stale or missing, FRED says nothing instead of filling the gap.

Next

More KyCast verification as storm days accumulate, and more of the above on the stream. Questions or town requests: ask FRED in the live chat.
