Read a weather balloon without a meteorology degree

A hodograph is a picture of how the wind changes as you go up through the air. Weather balloons measure it twice a day from about 90 US sites.

  1. Click anywhere on the map. You get the closest balloon site, each drawn as its own tiny hodograph.
  2. Hover any coloured line, dot or shaded area to see what it is.
  3. Click it to open a full explanation, with this site's real numbers.

Data: National Weather Service balloon soundings (public domain), served by the Iowa State Mesonet. Educational only, and not a forecast. For warnings use the National Weather Service.
