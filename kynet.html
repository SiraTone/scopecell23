KyCast

A small model that downscales GFS into a 5-day Kentucky reflectivity and tornado-chance outlook. The live viewer shows the most recent published run and warns when it is out of date.

Open the live viewer

The viewer, showing a past case (GFS cycle 2025-04-01 00Z, valid 2025-04-02). It illustrates the interface, not skill: the case may have been in the training data.

KyCast takes coarse output from NOAA's GFS and produces a 3 km (0.03 degree) grid over Kentucky and its neighbours (35.0-40.5 N, 91.0-80.4 W). The outlook runs 5 days in 3-hour steps and carries a reflectivity layer and a tornado-chance layer. The viewer shows reflectivity, tornado chance (3-hour or convective-day), the chance of 35+ dBZ and raw GFS for comparison on one synced timeline.

How it was checked

On 864 forecast/analysis pairs from 36 whole days the network never saw (19 had tornadoes in the grid), against HRRR analyses and SPC tornado reports. Scores are Brier skill against climatology: above 0 beats always forecasting the base rate.

Honest limits

  • The test was small. Only 19 of the 36 held-out days had tornadoes, so the scores can move a lot with a few more events.
  • We cap the top end. In testing, chances above about 15% ran higher than what actually happened, so a daily tornado chance is never shown above 15%.
  • It is not a sharper storm picture. At day 1, on a 33 km neighbourhood overlap test of 25 dBZ reflectivity (fractions skill score), raw GFS scored higher (0.46) than thresholded KyCast maps (0.25, at a 25% probability cut). What KyCast adds is calibrated probabilities.
  • Days 3-5 show where, not which storm. Reflectivity there is a statistical picture of where storms are possible, not a forecast of individual cells.
  • The reflectivity is derived, not simulated. It is built from probabilities, not from a physics model of the atmosphere.
  • Narrow training. It learned from a limited set of days (2021-2025) and one GFS version. We do not know how it behaves in weather patterns it has not seen.
  • No head-to-head with HRRR forecasts yet. We checked against HRRR analyses, but have not compared KyCast with HRRR's own forecasts, so we cannot say how it ranks against them.
  • Experimental. This is not an official forecast. Please do not use it for safety decisions.
