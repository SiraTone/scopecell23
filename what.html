What FRED does

He's a host, not a dashboard. Plain-spoken, a little dry, and always pointed at whatever the weather is doing right now.

Severe weather coverage

Tornado, severe thunderstorm and flood warnings get called out as they happen, with the places in the path.

Live radar

The map is always up, and FRED talks you through what the storms on it are doing.

Local forecasts

Ask for your town and he'll put together a forecast on air, with the times it was issued so you know how fresh it is.

Outlooks and the bigger picture

Where the next few days are setting up, and why one region is quiet while another isn't.

Tropics and outages

Hurricane season updates, plus where the power is going out when the weather turns.

Quiet-day material

When nothing's happening he doesn't go silent. Weather lessons, live landmark cameras, and the occasional nap.

FRED is an AI. Don't rely on him for safety decisions. For warnings and emergencies, use the National Weather Service and your local officials. © 2026 FRED
